**Turbo Racer** is a high-speed 3D sci-fi aeronautical tunnel combat and multiplayer racing game engineered by **Vinay (`@karalkar_vinay`) — V-FACTOR STUDIOS / TECHNEXUS-Tech**.

Pilot **32 bespoke weaponized spacecraft** across 5 generations through machined rotating turbine conduits, multi-chord security laser webs, dynamic closing bulkhead shutters, full-tunnel 36-beam laser lockdown barriers, and autonomous AI **White V-Hunter UCAV Drone Squadrons**.

## ✨ Key Architectural Highlights

Every spacecraft is procedurally constructed in 3D using custom aerodynamic airfoils, chiseled intake fairings, spinning compressor fans, dorsal gyroscopic singularity reactors, floating wingtip V-Bit stabilizers, and articulated 6-barrel rotary rail-cannons:
* **GEN 1 (`#01 – #07` | Strike Darts):** Ultra-nimble micro-interceptors (*Pulse Dart, Needle-X, Spark Mite, Ion Flea, Vesper Nano, Pebble Jet, Glint Runner*) with Neon Emerald Precision Optical Scopes.

* **GEN 3 (`#15 – #20` | X-Scissor Interceptors):** High-velocity forward-swept & X-wing interceptors (*Zenith Mark II, Aether Cruiser, Turbine Fury, Phantom Blade, Velocity Raptor, Starlight Viper*).
* **GEN 4 (`#21 – #26` | Cyber-Spoiler Siege Corvettes):** Heavy armored corvettes (*Solar Javelin, Nitro Falken, Bolt Striker, Hyperion Arrow, Goliath Heavy, Mammoth Siege*) with Futuristic Cyan/Crimson Vector HUDs.

### 🎮 2. Four Core Game Modes
1. **ENDLESS MODE:** Continuous high-speed survival with imperceptible distance acceleration, 500m exterior space-bridge parallax vistas, and 11 live-morphing color-fusion themes.
2. **SHIFT MODE:** Dynamic airflow vortex rings (**Cyan Pull-Fans** & **Crimson Push-Fans**) paired with colored logic gates:
   * 🟨 **GOLD GATE:** Freezes all tunnel turbines for `3.0s`.
   * 🟪 **VIOLET GATE:** Reverses upcoming turbine rotation.
   * 🟦 **CYAN GATE:** Triggers `2.5s` local time-dilation.
3. **SOLO MATCH (8–10P Cross-City Multiplayer):** Up to 10 pilots race simultaneously over WebRTC P2P (`STUN`/`TURN`). Win by crossing the target finish portal first or surviving as the last pilot standing.
4. **2v2 TEAM CLASH:** Team 1 (Cyan) vs. Team 2 (Crimson) with 3 shared team shields, live seat interchange (`1A, 1B, 2A, 2B`), and synchronized combo multipliers.

---

### 🤖 3. Predictive AI V-Hunter Drone Squadron & 36-Beam Lockdown Walls
* **Quantum Warp-Portal Arrival:** White V-Hunter UCAVs warp in 92m ahead through expanding cyan/violet portal rings.
* **Autonomous Boids & Dodge Brain:** Each drone flies an independent Lissajous + waypoint trajectory with Reynolds boids separation and reactive crosshair dodge reflexes.
* **Predictive Ballistic Lead-Targeting:** Drones calculate exact time-of-flight intercept vectors based on your ship's velocity and lock their 3D under-nose laser sight before firing.
* **2.0-Second Post-Fire Tactical Window:** After firing a plasma volley, a drone's lateral thrusters drop to `22%` speed for `2.0 seconds` (flashing amber), giving you a tactical window to lock your V-Sight and obliterate it (`+1,000 PTS` + `2.0s Hyper-Shield`).
* **Full-Tunnel 36-Beam Laser Lockdown Barrier:** Spawns at major milestones (`1500m, 2500m, 3750m...`) with a 160m obstacle-free clear zone. Align your V-Sight with the center **`SHOOT RED CORE`** button at `(0, 0)` and fire your Overcharged One-Shot to shatter all 36 lasers (`+1,500 PTS`).
